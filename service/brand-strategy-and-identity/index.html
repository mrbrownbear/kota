Our brand process

Every stage is built to uncover what makes you different, sharpen how you communicate it, and turn it into a system that works in the real world.

Discovery2 weeks

We start by building a clear picture of your world today, where you want to go, and what stands in the way. Through desk research and conversations with key stakeholders, we uncover the opportunities for your brand and align everyone around a shared direction.

Work involved

Stakeholder interviewsDesk researchBrand auditCompetitor & industry analysis

Brand workshops3-5 hours

Next, we’ll run collaborative workshops to unpack our findings, challenge assumptions, and explore the foundations of the brand together. Positioning, perception, personality, audience, ambition. The stuff that actually shapes how a brand feels.

Work involved

Deepdive sessionsPlayback presentations

Brand strategy3 weeks

This is where the direction gets defined. We’ll build a differentiated brand strategy rooted in what makes you valuable, memorable and hard to copy. Your positioning, messaging, mission, values and ‘Big Idea’ all start to come together here into a strategic foundation your marketing, website, sales and design can actually build from.

Work involved

Vision & missionBrand positioningValue propositionsBrand & product architectureBrand personalityBrand values

Tone of voice & copywriting2 weeks

A strong brand should sound as distinctive as it looks. We’ll define how your brand speaks across every touchpoint, from website copy and campaigns to sales decks and social posts. That includes voice principles, personality traits, vocabulary, and practical guidance your team can actually use. Clear. Consistent. Human.

Work involved

Tone of voice pillarsGlossary & vocabularyHow-to writing guides

Logo & icon design2 weeks

Whether we’re refining an existing identity or starting from scratch, we design logo and icon marks with motion baked into them from the start. Every shape, decision and detail should earn its place, based on the brand positioning. The result is a brand mark that feels distinctive and built to last.

Work involved

Logo designIcon designMotion designAsset folder delivery

Brand identity3 weeks

Now the brand starts to come to life visually. We’ll explore typography, colour, layout systems, imagery and graphic styles across multiple creative routes before developing a chosen direction further. The goal is a visual system that feels cohesive, recognisable and flexible enough to grow with you.

Work involved

TypographyColour paletteLayout stylesBrand visualsImagery usageTouchpoint examplesMotion design

Online brand guidelines1 week

Finally, we bring everything together into one clear, usable system. Your strategy, tone of voice and visual identity will live inside an online brand guidelines platform that’s easy to update, share and apply across teams. A practical, accessible toolkit with downloadable assets.

Work involved

Verbal guidelinesVisual guidelinesLayout guidelinesTouchpoint examplesDownloadable assets

FAQ's

Branding shapes how people recognise, trust, and remember you online. A strong brand creates consistency across your website, content, social media, and marketing, helping people understand who you are faster and why they should choose you. It also impacts performance more directly than people think. Clear positioning improves conversion rates, stronger messaging sharpens marketing, and a distinctive identity makes your brand more memorable in crowded markets.

Yes, but not in a “new logo equals better rankings” kind of way. Strong branding supports SEO by making your website clearer, more trusted, and more useful to the people landing on it. Clear positioning helps shape better page structure and messaging. A strong tone of voice makes content more engaging. Consistent brand signals across your website, social channels, PR and wider digital presence can also support recognition and authority over time, leading to LLMs and search engines pulling you into the conversation. Search engines want to serve results people trust. A strong brand helps you become one of them.

Brand strategy is the thinking underneath the visuals. It’s the process of defining what makes your business different, who you’re trying to reach, how you should position yourself in the market, and what your audience should associate with your brand. That can include positioning, messaging, audience insights, values, tone of voice, competitive analysis, and your wider brand narrative. A strong strategy gives every future decision more clarity, from marketing campaigns to website design.

Because most brands sound interchangeable. AI has made it easier than ever to produce content, but much harder to sound distinctive. A clear tone of voice helps your brand feel recognisable and human across every touchpoint, whether that’s your website, social content, sales decks, or campaigns. The right tone builds familiarity, trust, and emotional connection over time.

Your brand should evolve when the business outgrows how it currently presents itself. That could happen because your audience has changed, your positioning has matured, your offering has expanded, or your identity simply no longer reflects the quality of the business behind it. Not every brand needs a complete rebrand. Sometimes small strategic refinements create the biggest shift.

Branding affects more than vanity metrics. You can measure its impact through stronger conversion rates, higher-quality leads, improved engagement, increased brand recall, better customer retention, and clearer market perception. Often, teams also feel the difference internally, with sharper messaging, more confidence, and better alignment across sales and marketing. Strong brands reduce hesitation. That’s where the real value starts showing up.

Always. We believe the strongest creative work comes from clear strategic thinking. Positioning, messaging, audience understanding, and tone of voice help shape identities that feel distinctive for the right reasons, not just visually different. That’s the difference between decoration and branding.

Absolutely. Not every business needs a complete reinvention. Sometimes the strongest move is refining what already works, sharpening the positioning, modernising the identity, and building more consistency across the brand experience. The goal is clarity, not change for the sake of it.

A focused identity refresh may move faster, while a full strategy, tone of voice, and visual identity project will naturally take longer. We’ll always map out timelines clearly from the start so there’s transparency around every stage of the process.

We make sure your brand is built to be used properly in the real world. That usually means online brand guidelines, downloadable assets, practical usage guidance, and support applying the new identity across your website, marketing, campaigns, and digital touchpoints. We can also support with brand-to-build; creative website design, SEO & GEO, content, and ongoing creative rollout.
